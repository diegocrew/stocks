Full description:
Explore global stock markets with Stock Atlas, a native Android research dashboard.

YOUR WATCHLIST
Choose up to 30 companies and keep your selections saved on your phone. Browse the company directory and search by company name, ticker or country, with sector filters.

MARKET AT A GLANCE
See daily and weekly changes, market breadth and market-cap-weighted daily movement for your selected companies. Tap a performance tile to explore its price history.

PRICE HISTORY
View day, week, month, year and maximum available price history. Tap the chart to inspect individual observations. Quotes display their source timestamps and listing currencies.

DISCLOSED PORTFOLIOS
Research top disclosed holdings for SPY, QQQ, ARK Innovation and Berkshire Hathaway. Reported holdings may be delayed, and the dashboard covers only the displayed subset, not the fund's total return. Fund and company names identify data; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

No app account, advertisements, subscription or in-app purchases. Internet access is required for market data. Saved watchlists stay on your device; opening charts requests ticker-specific history from our public data host.

Market data may be delayed, incomplete or temporarily unavailable. Stock Atlas does not provide brokerage services, trade execution, personalized financial advice, or guarantees of investment returns. For research only.
